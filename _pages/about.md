I am a machine learning engineer and researcher focused on building reliable, production-ready ML systems. This blog chronicles the models I build, the experiments that worked (and those that did not), and the principles that guide my practice.

Expect to see:

- Concept walkthroughs that unpack dense papers and mathematics.
- Engineering guides for scaling training and inference pipelines.
- Reflections on responsible ML and human-centered evaluation.

When I am not tinkering with models, you can find me mentoring emerging practitioners and speaking at community meetups. Say hello on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sagarchapara/) or explore my [GitHub projects](https://github.com/sagarchapara).
